Tomato Early Blight

Alternaria solani
🍅 Tomato Severity: Moderate 97.4% Confidence

Observed Symptoms & Pathology

Dark brown spots with concentric target-board rings on older leaves. Yellow halos surrounding necrotic tissue.

Biological Causes & Risk Factors

Fungal pathogen surviving in crop debris. Spreads during warm temperatures (24-29°C) with persistent leaf wetness or overhead irrigation.

Agronomic Advisory

Recommended Actions for Farmers

Specific, crop-tailored emergency containment, organic remedies, and expert thresholds

Immediate Field Actions
  • Prune lower infected foliage immediately using sanitized shears.
  • Do not handle wet foliage to avoid transferring bacterial/fungal spores.
Organic & Bio-Control Options
  • Apply certified bio-fungicides based on Bacillus subtilis strains.
  • Use OMRI-listed copper octanoate foliar spray early in the morning.
Recommended Treatment Methods
  • Ensure thorough spray coverage on both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
  • Follow product-label directions and local extension guidelines; avoid inventing chemical dosages.
Irrigation & Fertilizer Management
  • Switch from overhead sprinklers to root-level drip irrigation.
  • Avoid excessive nitrogen which promotes soft, vulnerable tissue.
Long-Term Prevention Techniques
  • Implement a 3-year non-solanaceous crop rotation.
  • Apply organic mulch to suppress rain splash from soil.
When to Consult an Expert

Contact your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or extension specialist if symptoms expand across more than 15% of the total canopy within 48 hours.

Agronomic Notice: Any chemical treatments must strictly follow manufacturer product-label directions and local agricultural university recommendations. Never guess unverified dosages.
